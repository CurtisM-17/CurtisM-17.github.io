About Me

Hello! My name is Curtis and I'm a 22-year-old student in my second year of the Game Programming advanced diploma program at Humber College in Etobicoke, Ontario. I started learning how to program games when I was 12 through the Roblox platform, which I credit for giving me the skills and drive to dedicate my career to this industry ever since.

I'm excited to learn more about game development throughout my career, and my hunger for improvement means I'm always ready for feedback. I'm only satisfied when I'm proud of my work, and I value self-care to ensure I'm always at my best.

While most of my experience is in general game programming, I pride myself on my ability to learn quickly, pay close attention to detail, and adapt to new environments. I'm very passionate about the industry, not just as an appreciator of existing games, but also in excitement for the future. I believe that games are dialogues that connect us in powerful ways, and I believe that we are still only in the early days of a deeply powerful artform.

Outside of programming, I'm passionate about music, soccer, and playing new games. I strongly believe in empathy and inclusion, not just for the people around me but also for the people who experience my games.
